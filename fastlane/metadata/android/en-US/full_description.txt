OptiShare QR transfers files between devices using sequences of QR codes, without a network connection. Select a file, create a text note or capture a photo, then scan the displayed codes with the receiving device.

The transfer protocol compresses data with GZIP, checks packets with CRC32 and verifies the reconstructed file with SHA-256. XOR parity packets can help recover a missing data packet in a block. Transfers are saved locally in the archive, where files can be exported or shared.

The camera permission is used to scan QR codes. QR codes are not encrypted: anyone who can capture the sequence may be able to reconstruct its contents.
