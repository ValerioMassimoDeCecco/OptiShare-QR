OptiShare QR transfers files between devices using high-frame-rate QR code sequences (up to 120Hz VSync), completely offline without internet, Wi-Fi, or Bluetooth. Select single or multiple files (automatically bundled into a ZIP archive), create a text note, capture a photo, or generate universal standard QR codes for Wi-Fi networks and vCard contacts.

The transfer protocol compresses data with GZIP, supports optional AES-256-GCM password encryption (PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256), checks packets with CRC32, and verifies the reconstructed file with SHA-256. XOR parity packets and Reverse NACK QR codes allow fast recovery of any missed packets.

The camera permission is used exclusively offline to scan QR codes (with hardware torch and quick 1x/1.5x/2x zoom controls).
